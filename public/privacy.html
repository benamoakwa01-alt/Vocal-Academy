Privacy Policy

Pneuma Music Academy · Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information Pneuma Music Academy ("the Academy", "we") collects through our website and student portal at pneumamusicacademy.vercel.app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and login information.
  • Learning records: the courses and cohorts you are enrolled in, and the classes you attend.
  • Class recordings: live classes are recorded. A recording may include the video, voice and name of teachers and students who take part.

How we use it

  • To run your account and enroll you in courses and cohorts.
  • To let you and your classmates watch class recordings.
  • To contact you about classes, schedules and your enrollment.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

Where recordings are stored (Google Drive)

A copy of each class recording is stored in the Academy's Google Drive account so that it can be played back in the portal. Our app connects to Google Drive only to create and manage the recording files it uploads itself. It cannot see or open any other files in that Drive. The app does not access your personal Google account or your personal Drive.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the Academy, including Google (Drive storage), our video classroom service, and our website and database hosts. They handle information only to provide their services to us.

Sharing

Recordings and class information are visible to Academy staff and to students enrolled in the relevant course or cohort. We share information outside the Academy only when required by law or to protect the safety of our community.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while you are a student, and recordings for as long as they are useful for teaching. You can ask us to delete your information or a recording that features you at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian may make these requests for you.

Contact us

Questions or requests: pneumamusicacademyglobal@gmail.com

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
